Heart Disease & Hypertension

Cohort analysis reveals significant clustering of heart disease onset in patients with systolic pressure exceeding 140 mmHg. Age accelerates vulnerability beyond age 50, particularly when compounded by elevated serum cholesterol.

  • Peak Age Window: 55–72 years demonstrated the highest incidence.
  • Blood Pressure Correlation: Systolic hypertension showed a 2.8x higher observed odds ratio.
🩸

Diabetes & BMI Interplay

Elevated fasting blood glucose levels (>126 mg/dL) show strong collinearity with Body Mass Index exceeding 30.0 kg/m², highlighting the profound role of metabolic syndrome in glycemic dysregulation.

  • BMI Stratum: Obese cohorts accounted for over 64% of diagnosed diabetes records.
  • Sedentary Lifestyle: Inactive patients exhibited consistently higher average HbA1c proxies.
