THE PROJECT

About Health360

Healthcare risk and chronic disease analysis through interactive data visualization.

Problem statement

Heart disease, diabetes, and stroke place a growing burden on healthcare systems. Delayed identification of risk factors and difficulty interpreting complex patient data can hinder preventive care. Health360 explores demographic information, clinical indicators, reported symptoms, and lifestyle behaviours to identify disease patterns and groups with elevated observed risk.

The goal is to communicate useful, transparent analytical insights to support further investigation and preventive-care planning.

Key objectives

  • Explore prevalence of heart disease, diabetes and stroke.
  • Compare results by age group and BMI category.
  • Examine patterns in blood pressure, cholesterol and glucose.
  • Analyze reported smoking, alcohol use, exercise and symptoms.
  • Make visual findings accessible through a web interface.

Tools & methodology

  • Tableau Public: Interactive charts, dashboards and story.
  • Flask: Python web application.
  • HTML/CSS: Website layout and responsive styling.
  • Render: Public hosting (after deployment).
Important limitation: These visualizations show descriptive trends and associations, not validated individual medical predictions or proof of causation. Interpret any risk score according to the dataset's definition. Do not use this project for clinical diagnosis.

NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the Health360 analytics project.

What is Health360?

A Tableau-powered project analyzing healthcare and lifestyle data to explore chronic disease prevalence and risk-factor patterns.

Which conditions are analyzed?

The project focuses on heart disease, diabetes and stroke, alongside clinical and lifestyle indicators included in the dataset.

Can the dashboard predict a disease?

No. The published dashboard presents descriptive analysis and associations; it is not a validated medical prediction or diagnosis system.

Where does the visualization data come from?

Embedded charts load from the published Tableau Public workbook. The website itself does not maintain a separate live patient database.

Why is a chart not loading?

Confirm the Tableau Public link works, check the network, enable browser JavaScript, and try opening the view directly in Tableau.
